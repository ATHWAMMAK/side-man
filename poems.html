CRAFTING DEMISE

When you decide to kill the being of success, If you have the courage to amplify the evil you possess, You see every bit of competition as beings of slaughter, You force everyone around you to submissively falter, Pressure can also be used to crack as much as possible, Creating diamonds to destroy should not be dismissible, Chaos can be used as a tool to forcefully eliminate, Power is a means to an end to hopefully manipulate. Unintended consequences brought up by human curiosity, Death happening as a result of unbreakable audacity, Trapped in a hole and crippled by stoic confusion, Lied to and cheated and betrayed by delusion, Conditioned to waste and rot into compromise, Not being neurotypical enough to actually socialize, Thus ending up as a hopeless and suicidal loner, If only accidental destruction was dealt with sooner. Being a victim of destruction is a tough break, The feeling of hope after despair is partly fake, Surviving a wreckage is likely a trip to dystopia, Being skeptical is the newest form of phobia, Planning to survive only to succumb to a fail, A road to success cost by a necessitated bail, Starting with hope and ending up on your knees, Creating huge debts to be paying up with huge fees.

FATE

Undeniable, unavoidable and inescapable, Will always find you at most vulnerable, State of mind especially when desperate, No matter what your soul tries to regurgitate, Over and over again, redemption is out of reach, Even for the most resilient who are easy to teach, A lot of survival tricks up their long sleeves, Before their warrior spirit inevitably flops like leaves. Justice, torture, turmoil and heaven, All with different intentions driven, Are outcomes desirable or even not, Common among the huge lot, Of people in the giant and deep rat race, That we have been forced to forcefully embrace, Till the end of our very short livelihoods, And shoved down the throats of innocent broods. Deep in corners or even the most closed spaces, Is where this phenomenon embraces, Power strength and absolute dominance, Over every human’s conscience and relevance, To others around us and in orbit, Of our overall livelihood and habit, Behavior and patterns of different realities, Which defines human life authenticities.

LAW

To give someone a clear sense of direction, To decide the governing of people after an election, Is to impose a certain way of living, Is to make life hard and anything but forgiving, A false sense of protection and security, An empty promise of order justice and serenity, Following is the illusion of a non-existent intuition, Which only makes authority vision come to fruition. The stranglehold of a society decades long, The indoctrination that existed remains strong, In the realm of a natural order creation, The hierarchy of humanity loses appreciation, For things that are meant to unforcefully happen, As we are busy forcing false realities to sharpen, To make all that doesn't happen forcefully real, Living in a society devoid of invisible chains is a steal. That which is meant to create honesty, That which suppresses amnesty, Is usually followed and done without hesitation, Enough propaganda is meant to hide negative connotation, Towards the sad and destructive reality of conformity, All it is is a massive display of calamity, To protect those who do not possess self-agency, In situations of great grave and fatal emergency.

MORNING

The only time clouds open with grace, The drops of due grasses embrace, Birds sing with their voices high, The mighty wind blows with a gentle sigh, The sun glows strongly without hesitation, The flowers all revel in their glorious pollination, The animals all rise in sheer jubilation, The trees all pile up to form a nature-like nation. Optimism floats at a very fast pace, Among the jolliest individuals of the human race, Fatigue and bloom find a way to die, Similar to how truth overpowers a lie, The human body experiences rejuvenation and repair, With extreme elegance, style and flair, A chapter starts after a very abrupt end, An opportunity for fun and exuberance to blend. The past is gone, the present is here, Though the scars of yesterday might be severe, An opportunity rises for eternal happiness to arise, And for hardship and turbulation to meet demise, For thrills, rides and rowdy experiences to be magical, The moment must count, and the enjoyment will be radical, To curve out a life worthy of commemoration, And an experience of permanent immortalization.

NATURE

The embodiment of light beauty and existence, The conglomerate of preservation love and resistance, The order that exists to maintain health and happiness, The answer to dead ends and physical hollowness, The only law that should be followed and respected, The only form of life that should be dearly accepted, The reason to live and the motivation to multiply, A reality to protect and an entity to fortify. Humans animals and plants and the grounded matter, Lots of tools to make and objects there to scatter, To navigate this is to create an undeniable legacy, Without being at the mercy of a power infested majesty, Magic that exists without casting an imaginary spell, Adventure that involves leaving your own shell, Playing for the biggest privilege of getting to conserve, Doing everything to survive involves being ahead of the curve. Existing in human beings all to drive action, All for the purpose of avoiding cell rotting sanction, It is in us to make our lives better and malleable, For the sole purpose of making reality more tolerable, That which drives human actions and human thriving, All that makes it right and satisfactory to the soul and moving, Freedom is relative to those who dont strive to have it, Life is sweet for those who learn to love it.

PURPOSE: THE MAIN DRIVER OF EXISTENCE

Waking up every day working to serve a cause, Being strategic and dexterous enough to dodge every pause, Every goal planned and written for fruits to bare, Every built project and born child handled with care, Strong motivated and locked in with every single move, Rhythm drowns every single one of us in a groove, Intertwined are the forces of nature we thrive in, Created is the uneven playing field we strive in. Staying in the game long enough to complete a mission, Manifesting the future well enough to fulfil a vision, Crowning the state of constant excellence, The overwhelming novelty of great intelligence, The gift of life that is offered is precious, The spirit of raw toil and bravery is gracious, Sponsored by the passion and the will to win, Inspired to the point of avoiding hell inviting sin. To experience an endless streak of victory, A desire to keep your head down and make history, The inspiration to create and preserve civilization, Created and bred from subtle and relentless visualization, Walking into endless bliss and an ocean of gold, Fortified are the smart strategic noble and bold, Created for the will of God to fill and multiply, For all that is possible exists to inspire and unify.

STRENGTH

Soul binding, heart cleansing, mind refreshing, Body ironing, atmosphere cleansing, energy raising, Every component of life under the sun, That enables our human spirit to run, Under the worst conceivable conditions, Controlled by the most deplorable situations, In the most horrible existing prisons, Only then do we learn valuable lessons. To be a possession worthy body of steel, To be a whole emotion easy to feel, To prevent the adventurous nature from death, To enhance the admirable phenomenon of health, One must embrace both steel and iron, The mind, spirit and body of a lion, The predator aura will never ever fade, If matched well to the level of ‘spade to spade’. The animal instinct is never questioned, The violent predatory nature is never sanctioned, In a world of well-bound chemistry, And naturally occurring orchestry, Where survival and longevity are priority, Where we welcomingly embrace superiority, Of well-developed species and breeds, Of animals with different and unique creeds.

THE HUMAN RACE

As round as the earth in its finest form, Is the round-about as it is the norm, To view life as a fast-moving axis, Which serves as the main basis, For the struggle for supremacy and survival, Even for those who are considered primal, Creatures that are in harmony with nature, And are far beyond great in large stature. A never-ending road to the revel of glory, Is a long and potentially devastating story, For those who will be conquered by error, That slows you down and breeds horror, In their lives and invites horrible curses, In turn breeding contempt from the masses, And giving birth to mental instabilities, Leading to physical and damaging deformities. We are in this struggle together, Strength is dearly needed to gather, Stream in this nearly miserable existence, We need to build a base of resistance, Towards the sources of dampening life span, That easily emasculates a strong man, Into giving up and committing suicide, By killing his spirit and forsaking the ride.

TRANSFORMATION

To many a souls that feel completely broken, To many a minds that long to be awoken, A switch in the conscious that needs a little drive, A bee that longs for sustenance that leaves its hive, Closed corners are open in a chaotic heap of desperation, Great men are morphed in a sea of abundant inspiration, A hole in the crack causes complete demise of weakness, An ailment is designed solely to cure and prevent sickness. A long and winded sail to the fountain of youth, A tiring and lonely search for the unfiltered truth, What seemed impossible before is now not far away, All feelings of doubt fear and hesitation are now being led astray, The newest form of a man is cultivated in extreme conditions, The disbelief of a changed psyche disappears after long revisions, Restored repaired and revived as a strong entity, Strive to always reach your highest possible identity. A change of heart is the first step to take, Detachment from your previous self is the first decision to make, The ultimate form of rebellion is change, Anything that appears different is seen as strange, Strength can withhold any form of adversity, The process of being better is routed in diversity, Hang in there long enough to live a better life, Believe in yourself hard enough for success to be rife.
