A PRODUCT OF YOUR EXPERIENCED REALITY

The gates of agency and the privilege of free thought are only bestowed upon those who have not been broken by their life outcomes too much to destroy the chemistry of the body and the autonomy and efficiency of the mind. For every bully that pushed around, for every altercation that was bound, for every disorder that was found, for every extra added pound, was a destroyed state of being and a depressed body devoid of any functional chemistry. Childhood is meant to be full of optimism and wonder of how the future is going to hold up. Destroying a child’s psyche before maturity is death of the soul.

In contrast, a life rich in universal validation, an existence devoid of condemnation, a regular swim in appreciation and a deficiency in demoralization are all responsible for creating a feeling of exuberance, satisfaction and confidence. Walking forward knowing you are a victor in anything you do is key in creating the winning formula that leads many men to scorn and death in pursuit of it and in failure to attain it. Most human beings are just simple creatures longing for meaning in pursuit of happiness.

Conditioned for victory from infancy, avoiding the trap of a fallacy. Strong walls moved in spirit of bravery, the spirit of curiosity shielded from robbery. Raised in pursuit of the goal of nourishment, on the road of reaching the state of merriment.

TAINTED BY THE CURSE OF REJECTED AUTHENTICITY

Gone are the days of forging your own being and lying to your own soul in the exchange of belonging and validation. Run down is your willingness to stomach receiving treatment indifferent to that of your own expectations for being your unfiltered self resulting in the start of an undesired transformation that leads to detachment of your five senses from reality. Crowned from desperation is the heart-breaking tragedy that you call a day-to-day life that involves nothing but teeth displayed in hesitation, friendship forged by exploitation and motivation replaced with hollowness.

The last thing an individual needs is to go against his own senses, intuition and soul to fulfil the empty lives of those around him just to be noble in a world that has long suppressed the spirit of rewarding the good-hearted and the genuinely genuine and bathing them in appreciation and peace. A silver lining, however, has emerged from a seemingly distant cloud in the form of alienation of those who dare to go against their five senses to lie to the masses and display a dream version of themselves.

Crown the state of genuineness, clown the fate of hollowness. Thrive in the realm of wonder, strive for the strength of thunder. Frame the building blocks of your own body, tame the temptations even when they are crowdy. All this achieved produces and breeds a human ready for the trip of discovery, fun and adventure.

WISHFUL THINKING

When you wake up in the sea of your own delusion and fail to realize that dreams are only valid when they fester into reality, you will only set yourself up for a whole life inside the abyss of a world that only exists in a vision separated from a state of fruition. Destiny only belongs to those who are courageous enough to take action to write history as a part of a winning class that prioritized victory as the ultimate orchestra of existence. Hesitation of the mind is as poisonous as the orgasmic nature of the human consciousness to perceive heaven without any clear and concise intentions to alive it.

To be tempted and tried into a tumultuous trip and a telepathic train of thought is to tremble into a transformative transport into a town of tragedy. Avoiding such a prison is surrendering into a life of bliss, youth, curiosity and adventure. Nothing more beautiful than living a dream as opposed to dreaming of it. Nothing more tragic than missing out on wonderful experiences by settling for a life of strict imagination.

On the deathbed are many cries of regret and feelings of stinging dread. All this caused by a constant and crippling state of distance from the reality that surrounds. It is better to live a rich experience than live one cursed by stifling poverty.

SUMMED UP PERFECTLY

Rung up in the mind and narrated in privacy, this is one way of addressing serious issues without abandoning any needed point of reference.
