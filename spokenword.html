LIVING A LIE

-If you can find it within yourself to recognize the fact that perception and social norms are the main driver of modern existence, the soul finds its way into the bliss and escapes away from the horror of consequences that come with being oblivious to the fact that most of human existence in the modern world is based on an imaginary perception that lives in infantile minds. Something inside of all of us dictates when we are fed with the sour meals of deceit or the sweet and delicious delicacies of authenticity.

-Roaming in the roads of ravaging and rowdy rebels is a revealing revelation that re-enacts the reality of life. All that you think you know regarding freedom will blur the line between indoctrination and truth. It will teach you the ways of true living and it will reveal to you the trays of false existence. To get closer to the truth is to dismiss every paradigm that dictates otherwise.

-Embracing a state of being that only exists in fantasy, living long enough to reap the benefits of a forged legacy, running into deep problems that require the soul to speak, being derailed by the constant truth revealing stories that leak, your true self does not allow for a fabrication to form, not forming a strong enough character frame to weather the storm.

-Sad are the times that involve being destroyed by a contrasting reality to an imagined world. All that is valuable is never formed in a state of forgery.

SMILE THROUGH THE PAIN, DANCE THROUGH THE RAIN

-The walk of life is filled with many hurdles that harden the process of progress and the gravity of momentum that hastens the quality of life that we live. Many a times we experience moments that bring us back down to earth from the heaven that is delightful delusion. Moments built for memories, lessons meant to teach, setbacks that exist to be overcome are all ingredients that make life worth living.

-We all own the life building blocks that allow us to be destructive in the midst of obstacles created by the ignition of thin-air escalation. That allows us to find it in us to maneuver even the hardest of situations built to shatter hearts and break dreams. Each and every single one of us has the ability to make a broken ladder stable, to turn reality into a captivating fable, to overturn a long standing label, to become a strong entity and a dominant staple.

-Overturned and crucified are the long lasting burdens that held most of us back from living in bliss, reveling in freedom, swimming in melody and breaming with energy. Falling in an endless void of misery and isolation can only with a magnetic force of resistance that surpasses any and every challenge known to the human subconscious.

-Rowdy are the ones that have been conquered by long standing grievances with humanity to the extent of impatience. Angry are the ones that get consumed by their own dark desires to the point of forsaking their dreams. Blessed are the ones that find a path to their promised land and resurrect a giant boulevard of broken dreams.

SHARPENED TOOLS, BLOODY KNIVES

-To allow authority to run you over is giving energy to the rise of propriety to the highest echelon of the human food chain. The goal of human co-existence is to complement each other in every single department of life. Damned are all that believe the strangle holders of the system that rules and controls over an entire fleet of an innocent population for they will lose their humanity in the process.

-Made in the image of God are we not meant to waste in the confines of control and the dead end that awaits in the hell that is cooked in the kitchen that belongs to those who perpetrate suppression to conformity. Formed in the bubble that requires tolerance of the highest degree is the process that involves losing individuality, suppressing invincibility, aborting indefatigability and adopting infertility.

-Primed and ready for war, toiling and striving for more, all in the protection of an oppressive authority and an idea that exists in figuration. Rowdy for a non-existent course that never fulfilled any self-desire of the soul that cries out for amnesty and fulfillment. Crowded are the thoughts that never come to fruition and only stay in the confines of a mind beaten up to the state of crippling confusion.

-Having and possessing the tools to be sovereign and precise in the art of living free with relative ease is the by-product of being intelligent, calculated and tenacious to the max.
